Medical disclaimer

The Folded Mind and the NeuroPsych Atlas are for education. The information here does not replace medical diagnosis, treatment or the assessment of a physician, psychologist or other health professional, and must not be used on its own for diagnostic or treatment decisions.

Drug names, doses and treatments summarize findings from the scientific literature; they are not personal advice. If you are concerned about yourself or someone close to you, contact a health professional. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Information is given with its sources and reviewed by the author; errors or outdated information may still occur. If you spot a mistake, write to us and we will correct it.

Privacy

The site uses no cookies, has no accounts and collects no information that identifies you.

  • Your preferences (language, day/night theme, panel and atlas settings) are stored only in your own browser and are never sent to us. You can remove them by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts) and the 3D library (three.js; cdnjs and jsDelivr) are loaded from external servers. Like any web request, these servers can see your IP address and browser information.
  • The site is hosted on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Pages). To serve the site and protect it from attacks, Cloudflare briefly processes technical connection data such as IP address and browser information; this may also be processed on servers in the USA.
  • To see visitor numbers, an aggregate statistics tool that uses no cookies and does not identify individuals (Cloudflare Web Analytics) is used; it does not track individual visitors.
  • If you email us, we use your address only to reply; we do not share it with anyone else. Correspondence is processed on the servers of our email service providers abroad.
