Lateral Orbitofrontal Cortex

Cortex orbitofrontalis lateralis

Evaluates punishment and negative outcomes; signals that behaviour needs to change.

Open in the 3D atlas →All regionsParent structure: Orbitofrontal Cortex
AnatomyFunctionsConnections and chemistryClinicalIn the modelReferences

Anatomy

  • The lateral part of the orbital surface: HCP-MMP areas 11l, 13l, 47m and, in the model, the other parts of area 47 (Glasser et al., 2016).

Functions

  • Lateral orbitofrontal activity relates to evaluating punishers and to subsequent behavioural change (Kringelbach and Rolls, 2004; O'Doherty et al., 2001). B

Connections and chemistry

  • Caudate (OCD loop)
  • Amygdala

Clinical

  • OCD: beyond the classic cortico-striatal model, lateral and medial OFC, dorsal anterior cingulate and amygdala circuits are discussed; extinction learning is also impaired (Milad and Rauch, 2012). B

In the model

How this structure is defined in the 3D model, and the data source.

HCP-MMP1.0 (Glasser et al., 2016) areas: 11l, 13l, 47m, 47s, 47l, a47r, p47r. Each vertex of the region surface in the model was assigned to the area of the nearest HCP voxel; vertices outside this list joined the nearest sub-area along the surface, and the boundary is a smoothed iso-line. This is a group average: boundaries vary between individuals.
