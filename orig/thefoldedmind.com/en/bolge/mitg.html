Middle and Inferior Temporal Gyri

Gyri temporales medius et inferior

The temporal node of the distributed network for concepts and word meaning.

Open in the 3D atlas →All regionsParent structure: Inferior Temporal & Fusiform Cortex
AnatomyFunctionsConnections and chemistryClinicalIn the modelReferences

Anatomy

  • The lateral and inferior temporal surface: HCP-MMP areas TE1a, TE1m, TE1p, TE2a, TE2p, PHT, PH and the part of A5 in this region (Glasser et al., 2016).

Functions

  • Meaning: in a meta-analysis of 120 imaging studies, it is one of the nodes of a left-lateralized semantic network (Binder et al., 2009). A
  • Reappraisal: cognitive control may reduce amygdala activity by changing semantic representations in lateral temporal cortex (Buhle et al., 2014). A

Connections and chemistry

  • Fusiform gyrus
  • Pars triangularis (Area 45)
  • Temporal pole

Clinical

  • Schizophrenia: in ENIGMA, across 4474 patients, the cortex is thinner overall (d ≈ −0.53), with regional differences beyond this (van Erp et al., 2018); a group finding that does not diagnose an individual. A

In the model

How this structure is defined in the 3D model, and the data source.

HCP-MMP1.0 (Glasser et al., 2016) areas: TE1a, TE1m, TE1p, TE2a, TE2p, PHT, PH, A5. Each vertex of the region surface in the model was assigned to the area of the nearest HCP voxel; vertices outside this list joined the nearest sub-area along the surface, and the boundary is a smoothed iso-line. This is a group average: boundaries vary between individuals.
