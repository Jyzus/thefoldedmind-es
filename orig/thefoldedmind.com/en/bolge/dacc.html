Dorsal Anterior and Anterior Midcingulate Cortex

Gyrus cinguli, pars dorsalis et medius

Where negative affect, pain and cognitive control converge; the medial node of the salience network.

Open in the 3D atlas →All regionsParent structure: Anterior Cingulate Cortex
AnatomyFunctionsConnections and chemistryClinicalIn the modelReferences

Anatomy

  • Above and behind the genu: HCP-MMP areas p24, a24pr, p24pr, a32pr, p32pr, 33pr. In the model the cingulate motor areas (24dd, 24dv) are also in this part (Glasser et al., 2016).

Functions

  • Negative affect, pain and cognitive control overlap in the anterior midcingulate (meta-analysis: Shackman et al., 2011); differentiation of subregions is also argued (Vogt, 2005). A
  • Forms the salience network together with the anterior insula (Seeley et al., 2007). B

Connections and chemistry

  • Anterior insula (salience network)
  • Caudate (OCD loop)
  • Thalamus

Clinical

  • In pediatric OCD the error-related brain response (ERN) is large and stays large after treatment (Hajcak et al., 2008). B
  • Transdiagnostic grey matter loss: a meta-analysis of 193 studies highlights the dorsal anterior cingulate and anterior insula (Goodkind et al., 2015). A

In the model

How this structure is defined in the 3D model, and the data source.

HCP-MMP1.0 (Glasser et al., 2016) areas: p24, a24pr, p24pr, a32pr, p32pr, 33pr, 24dd, 24dv. Each vertex of the region surface in the model was assigned to the area of the nearest HCP voxel; vertices outside this list joined the nearest sub-area along the surface, and the boundary is a smoothed iso-line. This is a group average: boundaries vary between individuals.
