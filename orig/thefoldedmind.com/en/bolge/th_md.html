Mediodorsal Nucleus

Nucleus mediodorsalis thalami

The thalamic partner of the prefrontal cortex: carries out working memory, decision-making and reward learning together with prefrontal circuits.

Open in the 3D atlas →All regionsParent structure: Thalamus
AnatomyFunctionsClinicalIn the modelReferences

Anatomy

  • In the medial thalamus, next to the third ventricle, medial to the internal medullary lamina.
  • Reciprocally connected with the DLPFC, OFC and ACC; input: amygdala, ventral pallidum, SNr.

Functions

  • Working memory and cognitive flexibility: sustaining prefrontal activity.
  • Relaying reward and value information to the prefrontal cortex (limbic loop).

Clinical

  • Schizophrenia: reduced mediodorsal nucleus volume and differences in prefrontal–thalamic connectivity.
  • Contributes to memory impairment in Korsakoff syndrome.

In the model

How this structure is defined in the 3D model, and the data source.

Najdenovska et al. (2018) thalamic nuclei atlas (diffusion MRI clustering; ICBM 2009a symmetric template). Each voxel of the CerebrA thalamus was assigned to the most probable nucleus in these probability maps; because the atlas thalamus is about 20% larger than CerebrA’s, the borders were fitted to the shell (overlap of the two thalami: Dice 0.86–0.89).
