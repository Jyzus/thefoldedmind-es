Posterior Hypothalamic Nucleus

Nucleus posterior hypothalami

The nucleus above the mammillary bodies: involved in arousal and sympathetic responses; an experimental deep brain stimulation target in refractory aggression and cluster headache.

Open in the 3D atlas →All regionsParent structure: Hypothalamus & Pituitary
AnatomyFunctionsTreatmentIn the modelReferences

Anatomy

  • In the posterior hypothalamus, dorsal to the mammillary bodies, in the wall of the third ventricle; at the transition to the midbrain.

Functions

  • Arousal and sympathetic activity: raises heart rate, blood pressure and wakefulness.
  • One of the hypothalamic outputs of defensive and aggressive responses.

Treatment

This region from the perspective of medication, psychotherapy and neuromodulation. Letter badges are evidence levels.

  • Posteromedial hypothalamic stimulation for refractory aggression is experimental: the first two cases (intellectual disability) followed Sano's lesion target of the 1960s (Franzini et al., 2005); in a 7-patient series 5 improved markedly over the long term (Torres et al., 2021). C
  • Posterior hypothalamic stimulation in drug-refractory chronic cluster headache: 13 of 16 patients pain-free or almost pain-free at a mean of 23 months (Leone et al., 2006). C

In the model

How this structure is defined in the 3D model, and the data source.

Neudorfer et al. (2020) atlas of the hypothalamic region: hand-drawn at 0.25 mm on the average T1 and T2 template of 990 HCP participants (CC BY 4.0). The atlas is in the ICBM 2009b asymmetric template; it was transferred by world coordinates without nonlinear registration (centres within 1.0–1.7 mm of CIT168 for shared structures). The surface was smoothed while preserving volume. The nuclei were clipped to the outer boundary of the hypothalamus (the parent, from CIT168) with a 1 mm margin; this is a representational limit, and the part of the lateral hypothalamus extending beyond it is not shown.
