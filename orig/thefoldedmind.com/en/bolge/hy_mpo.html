Medial Preoptic Nucleus

Nucleus preopticus medialis

The nucleus at the front end of the hypothalamus: involved in parental care, sexual behaviour and temperature regulation.

Open in the 3D atlas →All regionsParent structure: Hypothalamus & Pituitary
AnatomyFunctionsIn the modelReferences

Anatomy

  • Behind the lamina terminalis, on both sides of the front end of the third ventricle; the medial part of the preoptic area.
  • A sexually dimorphic cell group was described in the preoptic area of the human hypothalamus: its volume in men is 2.5 times and its cell number 2.2 times that in women; between ages 10 and 93 its volume and cell number fall markedly. Its function has not been shown; it lies in an area essential for gonadotropin release and sexual behaviour in other mammals (Swaab and Fliers, 1985). B

Functions

  • Parental care: in mice, galanin neurons of the MPO govern pup care in both mothers and fathers; their ablation impairs care and their activation suppresses pup-directed aggression (Wu et al., 2014). C
  • Sexual behaviour and gonadotropin release (steroid hormone feedback).
  • Temperature regulation and sleep: warm-sensitive neurons of the preoptic area set body temperature (Morrison and Nakamura, 2019); the neighbouring ventrolateral preoptic nucleus is the switch that initiates sleep (Saper et al., 2005). B

In the model

How this structure is defined in the 3D model, and the data source.

Neudorfer et al. (2020) atlas of the hypothalamic region: hand-drawn at 0.25 mm on the average T1 and T2 template of 990 HCP participants (CC BY 4.0). The atlas is in the ICBM 2009b asymmetric template; it was transferred by world coordinates without nonlinear registration (centres within 1.0–1.7 mm of CIT168 for shared structures). The surface was smoothed while preserving volume. The nuclei were clipped to the outer boundary of the hypothalamus (the parent, from CIT168) with a 1 mm margin; this is a representational limit, and the part of the lateral hypothalamus extending beyond it is not shown.
