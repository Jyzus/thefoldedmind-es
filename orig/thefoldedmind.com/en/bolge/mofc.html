Medial Orbitofrontal Cortex

Cortex orbitofrontalis medialis

Tracks the value of rewards; guides choice by representing the expected outcome.

Open in the 3D atlas →All regionsParent structure: Orbitofrontal Cortex
AnatomyFunctionsConnections and chemistryIn the modelReferences

Anatomy

  • The medial part of the orbital surface: HCP-MMP areas OFC and pOFC (Glasser et al., 2016).

Functions

  • Medial orbitofrontal activity relates to reward value (Kringelbach and Rolls, 2004); monetary gain engages medial regions (O'Doherty et al., 2001). B
  • Prediction and evaluation of specific outcomes (Rudebeck and Murray, 2014). B

Connections and chemistry

  • vmPFC
  • Accumbens
  • Amygdala

In the model

How this structure is defined in the 3D model, and the data source.

HCP-MMP1.0 (Glasser et al., 2016) areas: OFC, pOFC. Each vertex of the region surface in the model was assigned to the area of the nearest HCP voxel; vertices outside this list joined the nearest sub-area along the surface, and the boundary is a smoothed iso-line. This is a group average: boundaries vary between individuals.
