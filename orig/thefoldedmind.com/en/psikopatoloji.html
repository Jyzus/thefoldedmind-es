Psychopathology by diagnosis

Pages that present each diagnosis through the brain’s circuits, networks and neurotransmitter systems, together with development and treatment.

Each diagnosis is also linked to Panksepp’s primary emotional systems, following the affective neuroscience perspective of the book The Green Walnut on the Couch. In the atlas’s “Diagnoses” tab, selecting a diagnosis lights up the related regions together; each diagnosis has four lenses: circuit, findings, symptoms and treatment.
