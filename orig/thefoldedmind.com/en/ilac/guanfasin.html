Guanfacine

α2A agonist (extended release)

Acts on a receptor, not a transporter: by stimulating α2A receptors on the dendritic spines of prefrontal pyramidal neurons, it is thought to strengthen the working-memory network (monkey studies).

Site of action

Target: Selective α2A agonist; cAMP falls, HCN channels on the spines close and prefrontal network connectivity strengthens (Wang et al., 2007). B

Not the flow but the signal at the target is strengthened: when the α2A receptor in the prefrontal cortex is stimulated, connectivity in the working-memory network strengthens (Wang et al., 2007). Also used as an add-on to a stimulant. B

Children and adolescents

  • FDA: ages 6–17, alone (2009) and as an add-on to a stimulant (2011). EMA: ages 6–17, when stimulants are not suitable.
  • Turkey: extended-release guanfacine has been licensed since 2023; according to the product information (KÜB) checked, it is indicated for ADHD at ages 6–17 when stimulants are not suitable, not tolerated or ineffective, as part of a comprehensive treatment program including psychological, educational and social measures.
  • α2 agonists: SMD −0.59 alone and −0.36 as an add-on to a stimulant; sleepiness is common (Hirota et al., 2014). A

Interactions

  • Broken down by CYP3A4: dose down to 50% with strong and moderate inhibitors and up to double with inducers (FDA label). A
  • Sleepiness adds up with sedatives and low blood pressure with other blood-pressure-lowering drugs; it is not stopped abruptly (rebound hypertension).
