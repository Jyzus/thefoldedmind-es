Library · medication · Antidepressants (SSRIs)

Sertraline

SSRI

An SSRI with large trials in pediatric OCD (POTS, 2004) and anxiety (CAMS, 2008); in these trials the best results came from combining it with CBT.

Site of action

Target: SERT blockade; occupancy 76–85% at the lowest effective dose (Meyer et al., 2004). In an in vitro comparison, the antidepressant with the strongest DAT binding (Tatsumi et al., 1997; clinical significance unclear). C

With the serotonin transporter (SERT) blocked, serotonin stays longer in the synapse: serotonin rises at the targets along the pathways leaving the raphe. SERT is densest in the raphe, thalamus, hypothalamus, striatum and amygdala (Beliveau et al., 2017). Emotional processing changes in the first week: the amygdala’s threat response falls before mood improves (Godlewska et al., 2012); the clinical response comes over weeks. B

Children and adolescents

  • FDA and EMA: OCD ages 6–17. Turkey: in the product information (KÜB) checked in October 2026, OCD in children older than 6.
  • POTS (OCD): remission 53.6% with combined treatment, 39.3% with CBT alone, 21.4% with sertraline alone, 3.6% with placebo. A
  • CAMS (anxiety): much improved 80.7% with sertraline + CBT, 23.7% with placebo. A

Interactions

  • Moderate, dose-dependent CYP2D6 inhibition: the levels of atomoxetine, aripiprazole and risperidone may rise. A
  • Itself broken down by CYP2C19 and CYP2B6; CPIC recommends genotype-guided starting doses. A
