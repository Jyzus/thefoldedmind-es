Atomoxetine

Selective noradrenaline reuptake inhibitor

Blocks only NET: raises noradrenaline and dopamine in the prefrontal cortex and leaves dopamine unchanged in the striatum and accumbens (animal data); considered to have low abuse potential, and its effect builds over weeks.

Site of action

Target: NET blockade (in vitro Ki: NET 5, SERT 77, DAT 1451 nM; Bymaster et al., 2002). B

Because NET also clears dopamine in the prefrontal cortex, blocking NET triples both noradrenaline and dopamine there; dopamine does not change in the striatum and accumbens (rat; Bymaster et al., 2002). The difference from methylphenidate: the pathways to the striatum stay quiet. B

Orange pulse in the atlas: noradrenaline and dopamine rising only in the prefrontal cortex

Children and adolescents

  • FDA and EMA: ADHD age 6 and over; also age 6 and over in the Turkish product information (KÜB) checked in October 2026.
  • SMD −0.56 on clinician ratings; lower than stimulants (Cortese et al., 2018). A
  • Boxed warning: suicidal thinking in short-term trials 0.37% vs 0% (Bangs et al., 2008). A

Interactions

  • Broken down by CYP2D6: strong 2D6 inhibitors (fluoxetine, paroxetine) increase exposure; the label advises a longer interval between dose increases, and genotype testing may be considered. A
