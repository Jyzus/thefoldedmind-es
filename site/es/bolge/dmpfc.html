Dorsomedial Prefrontal Cortex

Cortex praefrontalis dorsomedialis

Supports thinking about the mental states of others and of oneself; the anterior medial node of the default mode network.

Open in the 3D atlas →All regionsParent structure: Medial & Frontopolar Prefrontal Cortex
AnatomyFunctionsConnections and chemistryClinicalIn the modelReferences

Anatomy

  • The medial surface of the superior frontal gyrus: HCP-MMP areas 9m, 8BM, 10d and neighbouring dorsal areas (Glasser et al., 2016).

Functions

  • Theory of mind: the medial frontal cortex is consistently engaged when inferring the intentions and beliefs of others (Amodio and Frith, 2006; meta-analysis: Schurz et al., 2014). A
  • Appraisal and expression of negative emotion: dorsal medial regions are linked to this role, ventral regions to regulation (Etkin et al., 2011). B

Connections and chemistry

  • Default mode network
  • Posterior cingulate and precuneus

Clinical

  • A resting-state meta-analysis in depression reports increased connectivity within the default mode network (Kaiser et al., 2015). A

In the model

How this structure is defined in the 3D model, and the data source.

HCP-MMP1.0 (Glasser et al., 2016) areas: 9m, 8BM, 10d, 9a, 9p, 8BL, 8Ad, SFL, s6-8, i6-8, SCEF. Each vertex of the region surface in the model was assigned to the area of the nearest HCP voxel; vertices outside this list joined the nearest sub-area along the surface, and the boundary is a smoothed iso-line. This is a group average: boundaries vary between individuals.
