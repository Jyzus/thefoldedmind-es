Corteza premotora lateral

Area praemotoria lateralis

Planifica y selecciona el movimiento; incluye las áreas premotoras dorsal y ventral y el campo ocular frontal.

Anatomía

  • La parte lateral del área 6 y sus vecinas: áreas HCP-MMP 6a, 6d, 6v, 6r, 55b, COF, CPE (Glasser et al., 2016).

Funciones

  • Las áreas premotoras dorsal y ventral participan en distintos aspectos de la planificación motora y la toma de decisiones a través de redes corticales diferenciadas (Hoshi y Tanji, 2007; datos mayormente de monos). B

In the model

How this structure is defined in the 3D model, and the data source.

HCP-MMP1.0 (Glasser et al., 2016) areas: 6a, 6d, 6v, 6r, 55b, FEF, PEF. Each vertex of the region surface in the model was assigned to the area of the nearest HCP voxel; vertices outside this list joined the nearest sub-area along the surface, and the boundary is a smoothed iso-line. This is a group average: boundaries vary between individuals.
