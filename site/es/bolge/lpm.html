Lateral Premotor Cortex

Area praemotoria lateralis

Plans and selects movement; includes dorsal and ventral premotor areas and the frontal eye field.

Open in the 3D atlas →All regionsParent structure: Premotor Cortex & SMA
AnatomyFunctionsIn the modelReferences

Anatomy

  • The lateral part of area 6 and its neighbours: HCP-MMP areas 6a, 6d, 6v, 6r, 55b, FEF, PEF (Glasser et al., 2016).

Functions

  • Dorsal and ventral premotor areas take part in different aspects of motor planning and decision making through distinct cortical networks (Hoshi and Tanji, 2007; largely monkey data). B

In the model

How this structure is defined in the 3D model, and the data source.

HCP-MMP1.0 (Glasser et al., 2016) areas: 6a, 6d, 6v, 6r, 55b, FEF, PEF. Each vertex of the region surface in the model was assigned to the area of the nearest HCP voxel; vertices outside this list joined the nearest sub-area along the surface, and the boundary is a smoothed iso-line. This is a group average: boundaries vary between individuals.
