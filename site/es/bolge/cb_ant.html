Anterior Lobe (Lobules I–V)

Lobus anterior cerebelli

The sensorimotor anterior lobe of the cerebellum: the first somatotopic map of the trunk and limbs; regulates gait and posture.

Open in the 3D atlas →All regionsParent structure: Cerebellum
AnatomyFunctionsClinicalIn the modelReferences

Anatomy

  • In front of the primary fissure; lobules I–V of the vermis and hemispheres.
  • Receives input from the spinal cord (spinocerebellar tracts) and from motor cortex (via the pontine nuclei).

Functions

  • Gait, posture and limb coordination.
  • The first sensorimotor body map (continuous with lobules V–VI).

Clinical

  • Truncal and gait ataxia with damage to the anterior lobe (especially the vermis).

In the model

How this structure is defined in the 3D model, and the data source.
