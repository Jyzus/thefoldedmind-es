Diagonal Band of Broca

Stria diagonalis (Broca) et nucleus striae diagonalis

With the medial septum, the source of acetylcholine and GABA for the hippocampus: a basal forebrain nucleus that regulates the theta rhythm and the encoding of new memories.

Anatomy

  • A fibre band with its nucleus running along the posterior edge of the anterior perforated substance on the underside of the basal forebrain; its vertical limb (Ch2) rises towards the septum and its horizontal limb (Ch3) extends laterally (Mesulam et al., 1983).
  • Together with the medial septum (Ch1) it forms the medial septum–diagonal band complex (MS/DB), which sends cholinergic and GABAergic fibres to the hippocampus through the fornix.
  • The horizontal limb (Ch3) projects to the olfactory bulb (Mesulam et al., 1983).

Functions

  • Hippocampal theta rhythm: GABAergic fibres of the MS/DB target inhibitory interneurons in the hippocampus (Freund and Antal, 1988); theta is the "online" state of the hippocampus and provides a timing frame for memory encoding (Buzsáki, 2002). B
  • Encoding of new information through cholinergic input, spatial memory and exploration.
  • Processing of odour information and olfactory learning (horizontal limb).

Connections and chemistry

  • Hippocampus (fornix; septohippocampal pathway)
  • Medial septum (Ch1)
  • Olfactory bulb (Ch3)
  • Hypothalamus and preoptic area
  • Acetylcholine and GABA

Development

Developmental course through childhood and adolescence.

  • At 10.5 weeks of gestation a thin cholinesterase bundle appears running from the septum to the medial limbic cortex; at 15 weeks the medial septal, diagonal and basal nuclei segregate (Kostović, 1986).

Clinical

  • In Alzheimer's disease basal forebrain volume falls before that of the entorhinal cortex and predicts its degeneration (Schmitz and Spreng, 2016). The study measured the basal forebrain as a whole; the diagonal band is part of it. B

Assessment

Neuropsychological tasks and measures that probe this region's function.

  • Episodic and spatial memory tests
  • In research: hippocampal theta (iEEG, MEG)

Lesions and damage

Clinical findings expected with damage or dysfunction.

  • Basal forebrain damage (e.g., anterior communicating artery aneurysm) causes a Korsakoff-like amnesia; the septal nuclei and diagonal band are part of the damage, and the memory disorder is attributed to loss of input to the hippocampal system (Damasio et al., 1985). B

In the model

How this structure is defined in the 3D model, and the data source.

Neudorfer et al. (2020) atlas of the hypothalamic region: hand-drawn at 0.25 mm on the average T1 and T2 template of 990 HCP participants (CC BY 4.0). The atlas is in the ICBM 2009b asymmetric template; it was transferred by world coordinates without nonlinear registration (centres within 1.0–1.7 mm of CIT168 for shared structures). The surface was smoothed while preserving volume. The medial septal nucleus is not defined in openly licensed atlases, so the model contains only the diagonal band; pathways from the septum to the hippocampus start from this nucleus.
