Posterior Insula

Insula posterior

The primary map of the body's internal state: temperature, pain and visceral sensation arrive here.

Open in the 3D atlas →All regionsParent structure: Insula
AnatomyFunctionsConnections and chemistryIn the modelReferences

Anatomy

  • The posterior part of the insula: HCP-MMP areas PoI1, PoI2, Ig, PI and, in the model, neighbouring opercular areas (Glasser et al., 2016).

Functions

  • The first cortical representation of bodily sensation, becoming emotional awareness toward the anterior insula (Craig, 2009). B
  • Pain: blood flow in the dorsal posterior insula tracks reported pain intensity (Segerdahl et al., 2015); whether it is specific to pain is disputed (Davis et al., 2015). B

Connections and chemistry

  • Thalamus (VMb)
  • Anterior insula

In the model

How this structure is defined in the 3D model, and the data source.

HCP-MMP1.0 (Glasser et al., 2016) areas: PoI1, PoI2, Ig, PI, FOP2, FOP3, OP2-3, 52, RI. Each vertex of the region surface in the model was assigned to the area of the nearest HCP voxel; vertices outside this list joined the nearest sub-area along the surface, and the boundary is a smoothed iso-line. This is a group average: boundaries vary between individuals.
