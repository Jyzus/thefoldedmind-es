Frontal Pole

Polus frontalis (area 10)

Lets one goal be held pending while switching to another task; regarded as the top level of the executive system.

Open in the 3D atlas →All regionsParent structure: Medial & Frontopolar Prefrontal Cortex
AnatomyFunctionsConnections and chemistryDevelopmentClinicalIn the modelReferences

Anatomy

  • The most anterior tip of the frontal lobe: HCP-MMP areas 10pp, a10p and p10p (Glasser et al., 2016).

Functions

  • Switching to another task while keeping an unfinished plan in mind (Koechlin and Hyafil, 2007). B
  • Directing attention from external stimuli to internal thought and back: the "gateway" hypothesis (Burgess et al., 2007). B

Connections and chemistry

  • DLPFC
  • Frontoparietal control network

Development

Developmental course through childhood and adolescence.

  • Rostral prefrontal development is protracted: declines in grey matter and synaptic density continue into adolescence (Dumontheil et al., 2008). B

Clinical

  • The same review discusses rostral prefrontal findings in ADHD and autism; they have no diagnostic value (Dumontheil et al., 2008). C

In the model

How this structure is defined in the 3D model, and the data source.

HCP-MMP1.0 (Glasser et al., 2016) areas: 10pp, a10p, p10p. Each vertex of the region surface in the model was assigned to the area of the nearest HCP voxel; vertices outside this list joined the nearest sub-area along the surface, and the boundary is a smoothed iso-line. This is a group average: boundaries vary between individuals.
