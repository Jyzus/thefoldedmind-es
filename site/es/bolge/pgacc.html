Pregenual Anterior Cingulate

Gyrus cinguli, pars pregenualis

The cingulate in front of the genu: regulates the amygdala from above while resolving emotional conflict.

Open in the 3D atlas →All regionsParent structure: Anterior Cingulate Cortex
AnatomyFunctionsConnections and chemistryClinicalIn the modelReferences

Anatomy

  • In front of the genu of the corpus callosum: HCP-MMP areas a24, p32 and d32 (Glasser et al., 2016). Often called rostral ACC in the literature.

Functions

  • In resolving emotional conflict, the rostral cingulate reduces amygdala activity from above (Etkin et al., 2006). B
  • Ventral medial regions are linked to the regulation of negative emotion (Etkin et al., 2011). B

Connections and chemistry

  • Amygdala
  • Default mode network

Clinical

  • Depression: higher resting rostral ACC activity predicts treatment response in a meta-analysis (Pizzagalli, 2011); this is a group-level finding and is not used to choose treatment for an individual. A

In the model

How this structure is defined in the 3D model, and the data source.

HCP-MMP1.0 (Glasser et al., 2016) areas: a24, p32, d32. Each vertex of the region surface in the model was assigned to the area of the nearest HCP voxel; vertices outside this list joined the nearest sub-area along the surface, and the boundary is a smoothed iso-line. This is a group average: boundaries vary between individuals.
