Anterior Hypothalamic Area

Area hypothalamica anterior

The area between the preoptic area and the tuberal region: involved in temperature regulation and in defensive or aggressive behaviour.

Open in the 3D atlas →All regionsParent structure: Hypothalamus & Pituitary
AnatomyFunctionsLesions and damageIn the modelReferences

Anatomy

  • In the anterior (chiasmatic) region of the hypothalamus, behind the preoptic area and below the PVN.

Functions

  • Temperature regulation: part of the preoptic–anterior hypothalamic thermoregulatory centre (Morrison and Nakamura, 2019). B
  • Aggression: in hamsters vasopressin in the anterior hypothalamus increases attack and serotonin restrains it (Ferris et al., 1997). C

Lesions and damage

Clinical findings expected with damage or dysfunction.

  • Damage to the anterior hypothalamus can impair heat-loss mechanisms and cause hyperthermia.

In the model

How this structure is defined in the 3D model, and the data source.

Neudorfer et al. (2020) atlas of the hypothalamic region: hand-drawn at 0.25 mm on the average T1 and T2 template of 990 HCP participants (CC BY 4.0). The atlas is in the ICBM 2009b asymmetric template; it was transferred by world coordinates without nonlinear registration (centres within 1.0–1.7 mm of CIT168 for shared structures). The surface was smoothed while preserving volume. The nuclei were clipped to the outer boundary of the hypothalamus (the parent, from CIT168) with a 1 mm margin; this is a representational limit, and the part of the lateral hypothalamus extending beyond it is not shown.
